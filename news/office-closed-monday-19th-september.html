Office Closed: Monday 19th September 2022

On behalf of everyone at CaterKit, we would like to wish the Royal Family our heartfelt condolences following the death of Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II.

Like many UK businesses, as a mark of respect for Her Majesty, our office will be closed on Monday 19th September for the State Funeral.

We will be back open as usual on Tuesday 20th September.

We will still be responding to emergency breakdowns. Please call the office number on 01920 872580 and our on-call team or engineer will be able to help you.

Please be aware that we will not be monitoring emails during this time.
