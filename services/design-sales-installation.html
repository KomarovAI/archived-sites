Catering Equipment Design

CaterKit has been designing commercial kitchens for all hospitality and foodservice establishments for over 30 years. Whether you operate a restaurant, fast-food takeaway, local café, or staff canteen, we can work with you to design the optimum kitchen based on your operational needs and budget.

With access to the whole of the market, we can supply equipment from all UK and European manufacturers, including but not limited to Rational, Lincat, Falcon, Blue Seal, Foster, Hobart and more. So regardless of budget, we can develop a commercial kitchen design that meets all of your needs including aesthetics, if you’re considering a front-of-house kitchen.

We understand the importance of maximising space and efficiency within your kitchen, so we will work with you to provide detailed design and 3D visualisations to bring your project to life. This also includes bespoke stainless-steel fabrication, to ensure every inch of your kitchen has been maximised.

Our experience extends to complete installation and project management, so you can be confident that our commercial kitchens will be compliant and fit for use.

Catering Equipment Installation

Our goal at CaterKit is to be as flexible as possible. That is why we can offer an “installation only” service.

So, if you have purchased equipment elsewhere, new or second hand, or re-purposed equipment from a closed site, we can install this catering equipment, commission it, and provide the appropriate certification.

And if you are unsure of the history of the equipment, particularly with used appliances, we can provide a fully comprehensive service to get the equipment running like new. Find out more about our PPM, Servicing and Maintenance.

Our installation services start from just £160 + VAT and can include the extension of building services such as electricity, gas, water, and waste. We can also provide extraction installation and servicing to ensure your commercial kitchen is sufficiently set-up to accommodate your new equipment.

Occasionally, you may need to decommission your commercial kitchen equipment or remove it from the site.

With expertise across our group of companies working in basement kitchens or on the 52nd floor of The Shard, we have the necessary experience to handle your equipment with the utmost care.
