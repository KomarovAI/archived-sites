Takeaway Kitchen Design

If it’s time to renovate in order to create a kitchen that’s designed around your takeaway’s needs, our experienced designers will create something that’s fit for purpose and makes the best use of the available space. A good design will result in an ergonomic kitchen that is more comfortable to work in and could even allow you to expand your offering without the need for a larger kitchen or more staff.

The right design will also be key to offering flexibility to your menu, whether operating your own takeaway or dark kitchens; this will enable you to attract a diverse range of tenants or partner brands.

Versatile and multi-use equipment means your kitchens can offer a wide variety of dishes, without having to replace equipment with each menu change, saving time and money.

Our experts have many years’ experience designing modern, functional and hygienic kitchens that are created around each of our client’s specific needs. We will create a commercial kitchen for your takeaway that will help you increase productivity and quality at the same time as optimising the working environment for your staff and chefs.

Takeaway Kitchen Maintenance

The worst thing that could happen is a piece of equipment breaks down in the middle of a busy Friday night service. One way of preventing this would be Planned Preventative Maintenance of your takeaway kitchen equipment. Regular servicing will ensure your equipment is working properly and will also flag up any potential problems, giving you the opportunity to repair or replace at a time that suits you – not on a Friday night when the phones are ringing off the hook or the web order receipts are printing!

Contact us to discuss our maintenance options to ensure your takeaway service is less likely to be interrupted by frustrating breakdowns.
