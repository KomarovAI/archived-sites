Healthcare Catering Equipment Repair

Any catering equipment or warewashing equipment breakdown is unwelcome. When you’re catering for sick patients, hard-working healthcare staff and other key workers, not being able to use equipment will affect the service you can provide for them. 

With an online booking system and response times from just 2 hours*, we’ll have you up and running as soon as possible. 

Our engineers are all DBS-checked and have years of experience repairing all types of commercial kitchen equipment such as ovens, hobs, griddles, refrigerators, dishwashers, and even large cold rooms. We have a first-time fix rate of 89%, meaning we can get you back to your full-service offering as quickly as possible. 

Book your callout online or, in an emergency, phone our service desk team on 01920 872580.

Manufacturer Approved Repairs

We are an approved repairer for all the major catering equipment and wareashing manufacturers. We will therefore repair your kitchen equipment to the manufacturer’s standards without voiding your warranty or guarantee.

For equipment that is out of warranty, we provide a Callout Guarantee. This offers 10% off replacement parts if we cannot repair the problem on the first visit. If your equipment is unfortunately beyond economic repair, we will offer your healthcare facility or hospital a discount on new equipment. 

We are also a Rational Service Partner and will repair your Rational equipment with all the expertise of the manufacturer.

As prevention is better than cure, talk to us about our fully comprehensive Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM) service. By inspecting your kitchen equipment and carrying out any maintenance work needed around your schedule, we’ll make sure your equipment continues running as efficiently as possible, without any breaks in service caused by preventable faults.

Healthcare Centre Kitchen Design

If the time has come to refurbish the kitchen at your healthcare centre or hospital, our specialist designers will be happy to help. They will design a cost-effective and hygienic kitchen based on your specific needs to make sure it not only makes the most efficient use of the available space but is also comfortable for your staff to work in. 

By using appliances that have been designed for multiple functions, you can save space by reducing the equipment you need at the same time as being able to provide a healthy and more flexible menu for patients.

We will also design your kitchen to have separate areas that can safely accommodate the preparation of meals for patients with allergies. Hygienic design and equipment is our priority.

Our experts have many years of experience designing modern, functional and hygienic kitchens that are created around your specific needs and budgets, enabling your healthcare centre to make the most of your bulk-cooking kitchen facilities.

Healthcare Centre Kitchen Maintenance

With at least three meals a day to produce for possibly hundreds of people, the last thing you need is for a piece of catering or warewashing equipment to break down. One way of preventing this would be Planned Preventative Maintenance of your healthcare centre’s kitchen equipment. Regular servicing will ensure your kitchen equipment is working properly and will also flag up any potential problems, giving us the opportunity to repair or replace it at a time that suits your busy schedule.

We have a flexible schedule, and our engineers are willing to work whenever they are needed in order to service and maintain equipment with minimal disruption to the day-to-day running of your kitchen. 

Contact us to discuss our maintenance options to ensure your meal preparation is less likely to be interrupted by frustrating breakdowns.
