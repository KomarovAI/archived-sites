Canteen Kitchen Equipment Installation

We also offer a stand-alone installation service, which gives you the freedom to source your own equipment, whether you buy new or second-hand. 

Ask our experienced team of installation experts about our high-quality ‘installation only’ service. 

We’ll initially conduct a thorough pre-installation survey to ensure everything, including the services (gas, electricity, water), are fit for purpose. If anything isn’t fit for purpose, we’ll discuss what is needed in order to install your equipment safely and will provide a comprehensive breakdown of parts and costs. 

Then the installation will be handled by our experienced and qualified engineers. We are flexible enough to work around your schedule, including doing work out of hours, which means minimal disruption to the day-to-day running of your canteen’s kitchen. 

Finally, we’ll provide you with the relevant commissioning certificates.

Staff Canteen Kitchen Equipment Maintenance

The last thing you need is for a piece of catering equipment to break down just before the lunchtime rush. Scheduling regular Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM) of your canteen’s kitchen equipment is vital to ensure the reliability of your service. 

Regular servicing will ensure your kitchen equipment is working properly and will also flag up any potential problems, giving us the opportunity to repair or replace it at a time that suits your busy schedule.

We have a flexible schedule, and our engineers are willing to work whenever they are needed in order to service and maintain equipment with minimal disruption to the day-to-day running of your kitchen. 

Contact us to discuss all our PPM options.
