Booking repair and breakdown callouts are now even easier with CaterKit…

We have recently just launched a new online booking system that makes it easier to book emergency breakdown and repair callouts for your busy commercial kitchens.

The process is now more user-friendly, with a clear pictorial layout making it much quicker and easier to choose the service required. The improved booking also gives a clear indication of the minimum callout fee upfront, plus any changes in the callout fee when you have chosen your preferred date and time. So there should be no nasty surprises at the end of the booking process.

The new system allows you to book repairs at a time that suits you, so you don’t have to leave the kitchen during busy periods. Or alternatively, it’s easy to book at the end of service at a time that suits you. You don’t have to wait until our office is open.

Our online booking service has proved to be extremely popular, and the improvements to its design will make the process quicker and even more convenient.
