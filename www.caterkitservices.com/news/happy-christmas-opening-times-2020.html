Since we re-launched CaterKit in September 2020, we have been overwhelmed by the response shown to us by our customers both new and old.

2020 has clearly been a challenging time for all involved in the hospitality industry including the entire supply chain so we would like to take this opportunity to express our gratitude for the support of our customers and suppliers throughout the year.

We are confident that 2021 will be more positive for all!

The entire team at CaterKit would like to wish you a very Happy Christmas and Prosperous New Year. We hope that you will be able to enjoy a well-deserved break, spending it with friends, family and overindulging in all that Christmas brings.

Likewise, we will be having a break over the Christmas period. We will have adjusted opening and closing times from Thursday 24th December through to Monday 4th January, when we will be open as usual.
