Bespoke stainless-steel catering fabrication can help you to maximise space within your commercial kitchen and is more cost-effective than you think.

Whether you require a simple worktop food preparation counter or bespoke bar for front-of-house, we can work with you to design, supply, and install all your fabrication needs.

We also specialise in fabrication for difficult to access areas, such as basement kitchens and modifications to existing fabrication to help your equipment fit. Contact us to discuss your catering fabrication requirements.

Bespoke Fabrication to Maximise Hygiene

By choosing a bespoke stainless-steel fabrication service, you can also ensure that hygiene is maximised within your kitchen, crucial in today’s “new normal” and post-COVID world.

Our stainless-steel catering fabrication is always designed with hygiene in mind, ensuring that there are no gaps or voids where dirt, foodstuffs and liquids will collect.

Not only is this inherently more hygienic, but, quicker and easier to clean because a simple wipe down is required which can even be done during service.

Our hygienically sealed fabrication, using upstands and silicone against the wall, means that dirt ingress behind equipment or worktops is minimised, preventing the need to move it to clean behind.
