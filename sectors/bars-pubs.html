Bar & Pub Catering Equipment Repair

Our team of highly experienced and manufacturer trained catering equipment engineers can repair all of your equipment, from cooking to all refrigeration, ice machines, glasswashers and dishwashers.

And with food-led pub and bars rising in popularity, we understand how important your kitchen catering equipment is to your customer service. Broken down equipment can limit your menu and mean customers leave.

That is why we provide you with the ability to book your pub or bar equipment repair online, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week at a time that suits you. Suffered a breakdown at the end of service? You can book a repair before closing, knowing the equipment is on its ways to being repaired.

And with our Callout Guarantee, you can be sure that you will never pay more than you need to. With response times from 2-hours*, book your callout online to get your repair underway.

Pub Kitchen & Bar Design

It is important that your pub kitchen can cope with the demands of changing covers throughout the day and a varied menu. We have over 30 years’ experience designing commercial kitchens and bars across the pubs, bars and night-time industries.

We will work with you to understand your food offering, menu and kitchen space to design a pub kitchen that maximises space, utilises flexible equipment and is an efficient environment for your chefs and team.

We are an entirely independent company, so can supply and install equipment from major UK, European and US manufacturers ensuring that we can supply pub and bar kitchen equipment that is specific to your needs and budget.

Our service also includes the design and installation of bespoke stainless-steel fabrication for the optimum bar, ensuring that we can maximise space and create a front-of-house bar that is as unique as the space you want to create.

What is more, our design will focus on being as energy-efficient and hygienic as possible, reducing cleaning times and costs.

Pub & Bar Equipment Maintenance

We believe that prevention is better than cure, especially in busy pub kitchen or bar environments that must cope with large numbers of covers throughout the day, and demanding customers.

That is why we can support you with a range of ongoing Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM) contract options to ensure the reliability of your pub catering equipment.

Whether you require a one-off maintenance check for your Rational Combi Oven just before a key event, or a full refrigeration check before the peak Christmas period, we can help. Or if you want a full kitchen maintenance contract at a frequency that is in line with the demands placed on your pub kitchen, we can work with you to determine the right frequency.

Please do not hesitate to get in touch to discuss your options further.
