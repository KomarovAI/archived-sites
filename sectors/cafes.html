Café & Coffee Shop Equipment Installation

If you have already purchased your coffee shop equipment, such as coffee machine, refrigeration display counters, or café cooking equipment, we are happy to provide an installation only service.

Starting with a pre-installation survey, we will ensure that the space and services (such as gas, electric and waste) are fit-for-purpose. If they are not, we can provide a comprehensive cost-breakdown of the installation including plumbing and electrical work.

Our team of experienced engineers will then install and commission your café catering equipment ensuring it is safe and ready to use.
