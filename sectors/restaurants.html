Restaurant Kitchen Equipment Installation

We know that you may need to purchase new equipment elsewhere or prefer used equipment based on budgets. That is why CaterKit offers an equipment installation service, whether you have purchased from us or not.

The process can begin with a pre-installation survey, to ensure that space and your services are fit for purpose. 

If they are not, we can prepare all the appropriate mechanical and electrical services including gas, water, and waste. As well as modify existing kitchens including fabrication to make the perfect fit. 

Our team of experienced engineers will then install and commission your kitchen catering equipment ensuring it is safe and ready to use.
