Gas Safe Engineers

We will only send our engineers that are Gas Safe registered*. The Gas Safe Register is the official list of gas engineers, including commercial catering gas engineers for both natural gas and LPG. By law all gas businesses and their engineers must be on the Gas Safe Register to legally work on your equipment and issue a CP42 Gas Certificate. Our catering gas engineers are certified to work on “commercial catering”, “gas installation pipework” and “pipework testing and purging”.
