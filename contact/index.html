    About CaterKit Services Ltd

    CaterKit has been operating out of its Hertfordshire base for over 30 years. As a Rational Service Partner, we have the expertise and experience to support all hospitality and foodservice clients across Hertfordshire, London, Bedfordshire, Cambridgeshire, and the Home Counties.

    Our expertise extends far beyond Rational, with the ability to support all hot, cold, and washing equipment found in a commercial kitchen environment. Whether that is a gas oven range or walk-in cold room, ice machine or pot wash.

    We believe in transparency, convenience, and that speed is of the essence. That is why we have developed our online booking system and pricing structure to give you the ability to book anytime, anywhere, and a cost that is fair.

    Our Group of Companies

    CaterKit belongs to the same family of companies as TAG Catering Equipment and P&S Williams Refrigeration.

    TAG Catering Equipment specialises in the design and installation of bespoke kitchens and cook suites for fine-dining, Michelin-starred restaurants and 5* hotels.

    P&S Williams Refrigeration has expertise in all cooling, including hospitality refrigeration, freezers, ice machines, and HVAC.

    All companies work synonymously, so if you don’t feel that CaterKit is the right fit for you, or you require certain specialist expertise, we have a team or company that can help.
